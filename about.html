Curiosity, with a framework.

ForexLab is an independent application for exploring forex markets through data, structured analysis and paper-only experiments.

The purpose

Build understanding. Make uncertainty visible.

The project brings market-data validation, technical indicators, historical evaluation, AI-assisted analysis and simulated risk decisions into a local research workflow.

Its purpose is to help investigate how an idea behaves under stated assumptions. It does not establish that any strategy will be profitable or appropriate for a particular person.

Non-negotiable boundaries

Research stays research.

  • Broker order submission is disabled, including demo orders.
  • Unverified observations remain separate from accepted research data.
  • External demo data requires explicit authorization and data rights.
  • No claim of regulatory approval or verified live trading.

Development, not a launch claim.

Features continue to evolve. Availability of an integration does not mean a provider is authorized, connected or supplying verified current data. This site publishes no private research or account information.
