In development

A connected research toolkit.

Purpose-built capabilities for asking better questions about forex data—not promises of better returns.

◈

Source-aware market data

Distinguish synthetic examples, licensed historical imports and explicitly authorized read-only demo observations. API access never substitutes for data rights.

◷

Timestamp validation

Compare provider event time with local receipts. Preserve raw values and quarantine stale, future or uncertain timestamps instead of guessing an offset.

⌁

Technical market analysis

Study RSI, EMA, MACD and ATR alongside clear data provenance and assumptions. Indicators describe inputs; they do not guarantee outcomes.

⊞

Historical research

Evaluate ideas using chronological backtests, cost assumptions, simple baselines and out-of-sample validation. Historical evaluation is not future performance.

✳

AI-assisted investigation

Use model evaluations and reliability diagnostics as part of the research process. Keep uncertainty, limitations and comparison baselines visible.

◇

Paper trading & risk controls

Explore simulated positions, position sizing, stop-loss rules, daily loss limits and emergency stops. Execution stays internal and simulated.

This is a public project overview. The research application, private records and provider credentials are not part of this website.
