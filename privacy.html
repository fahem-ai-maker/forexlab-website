Privacy, without the guesswork.

What this public website does—and does not—collect.

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Scope

This policy describes the static ForexLab public website. It does not describe private research records, broker services or a separately operated research application.

No account or financial-data collection

This website has no registration, contact form, payment flow or broker login. It does not request passwords, tokens, account identifiers or trading records. No analytics or advertising scripts are included.

Language preference

If your browser allows it, choosing English or Arabic stores only that preference in localStorage under forexlab.website.language. It stays in your browser and is not sent to ForexLab. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data settings. Language switching also works without storage.

Hosting and technical requests

When published on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy practices. This site cannot promise that hosting creates no logs. All site fonts and visual assets are local; no third-party market-data requests are made.

External contact links

Following the repository or issue-tracker link takes you to GitHub. Information you publish there is governed by that service’s policies and may be public. Do not include sensitive financial or account information.

Questions and changes

Use the Contact page for questions about this public website. We may update this policy as the website changes; the effective date will be shown on this page.
