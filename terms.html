Project status

ForexLab is under development. This static website describes a research and paper-trading project; descriptions may change as development continues. It is not a hosted trading application or a commitment that every integration is available.

The content is for general information and research education. It is not personalized investment advice, an offer to invest, a trading recommendation or a request to transfer funds.

No broker orders—real or demo—are submitted through this website. Paper results, historical tests and AI analyses do not establish future results. No profitability, verified live-trading performance or regulatory approval is claimed. Forex trading can involve substantial loss.

Responsible use

Do not submit credentials, private account information or financial instructions through public project channels. External services, including GitHub, operate under their own terms. Verify information independently before relying on it.

Availability and contact
